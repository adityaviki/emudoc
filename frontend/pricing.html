How it is counted

Pages

A page of a PDF, a scan or a presentation counts once, when the document is first read. A document without pages, such as a Word file or a spreadsheet, counts a page for every $chars_per_page characters of its text. The sample project counts against nothing.

Questions

A question counts as it is asked, and trying its answer again counts no other. Pages and questions count from the first of each month, in UTC.$packs

At a limit

A request past a limit is refused with a sentence that says what lifts it, and owners and admins are told when the month's pages or questions reach 80 %.

Moving to a smaller plan

An organisation keeps everything it holds. Past what the smaller plan allows, it is read-only until it is within it again: its members read, ask, download and delete, but add and change nothing.
