# Service contract

Between Acme Logistics Ltd, the supplier, and Globex Supplies GmbH, the customer, signed in
Hamburg on 1 March 2026.

## 1 Services

Acme delivers pallets of office supplies to the customer's warehouse in Hamburg every Tuesday,
at the prices of the price list in force on the day of the order.

## 2 Late delivery

A delivery that arrives more than two working days late earns the customer a credit of five
per cent of the delivery's value, set against the next invoice.

## 3 Invoices

Acme invoices each delivery within seven days. The customer pays within thirty days of the
invoice date.

## 4 Termination

Either party may end this contract by giving ninety days' written notice to the other. Notice
is given by letter or by email to the addresses on the first page.
