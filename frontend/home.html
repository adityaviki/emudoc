Why Emudoc

Emudoc does not ask for your trust. Every claim points to the passage it came from, so checking an answer takes a click, not an afternoon.

  • Cited to the passage Not the document, the very words, on their page.
  • Across the whole project The invoice read against the contract, the log against the email.
  • Private to your team Never sold, and never used to train AI models.

Ask anything

The questions that used to take an afternoon

Emudoc reads documents against each other: the invoice against the contract, the delivery log against the email, the meeting notes against both. Before it answers, it reads each document's outline, searches its passages and looks up the facts they state, and then it shows its working.

  • Ask about the whole project, a folder, or just the documents you pick.
  • Every new chat suggests questions drawn from the project's own documents.
  • Chats stay private until you share them with the project.

Everything you need to get through the paperwork

Answers that show their sources

Find it by its words or its meaning

Search by the exact words or by what a passage means, in any language. Narrow by type, date or folder, search within the results, or across every project at once.

late delivery credit

A library that stays in order

Folders, favourites and recent files. Each document's title, type and date beside what the reading found, with its versions and its copies in other projects.

Take the answer with you

Download an answer or a whole chat as Word, PDF or Markdown, its citations as numbered notes. Export a project whole, originals, outline, facts and chats.

DOCX PDF MD

Scans and photos, read too

A contract that went through a scanner, or a photo of a delivery note, is read like any other file, and its answers cite it the same way.

Work on it as a team

Invite colleagues as owners, admins, members or viewers, keep a project private to the people you choose, and share a chat with the project when it is ready.

Read the facts into a table

Dates, amounts, parties and lines, read into a table you sort and filter, each cell with the words it came from. Extraction templates say which fields to read from each kind of document, and a corrected value is kept.

Check one document against another

Cross-checks compare an invoice with its purchase order or a price with the price list, and say where they differ. Automations run them as documents arrive, and reviews keep who approved what.

Write from what you found

A dispute letter, a reply or a memo, drafted from the project's documents with its citations, edited in versions and downloaded.

Ask the same questions every week

Save a project's questions as a set, asked on a schedule or as documents arrive, each run a report of the answers.

Talk it over beside the passage

Comment on the words you chose in a document, mention a colleague, and read what was done in the project lately.

Security and privacy

Your documents stay yours

What you upload is seen by the people you choose, and used for nothing but your work.

  • Never used to train AI

    What you upload and ask is used to run the service for you and for nothing else: never sold, never shown to other customers, and never used to train AI models.

  • Seen by whom you choose

    Roles from owner to viewer, private projects, and chats private until shared. An organisation can require two-factor sign-in of everyone in it.

  • On the record

    The audit log keeps who did what in the organisation, and a project or an account can be exported whole, or deleted.

  • Kept apart, and encrypted

    Every request is checked against the organisation it is for, and connections to the service, and from it to anything outside its own network, are encrypted.$kept
