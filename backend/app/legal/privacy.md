# Privacy policy

Version of $published.

This policy says what personal data Emudoc keeps, why, for how long, who else receives it, and
what you can ask of us. $operator ("we", "us") runs this Emudoc service and is responsible for
it. Write to $contact about anything in it.

## Who decides about which data

We decide about the data of your account: who you are, how you sign in, and what the service
records to keep it secure. The documents in an organisation's projects, and the questions asked
and answers given about them, are the organisation's: it decides what goes in, and we process
them on its behalf, as section 6 of the [terms of service](/terms) describes. If an
organisation uploaded a document about you, ask the organisation first; we help it answer you.

## What we keep, and why

- **Your account**: your email address, your name, your password (as a hash only, from which
  it cannot be read back), whether you confirmed your address, the Google or Microsoft account
  you sign in with, and with two-factor sign-in your authenticator's secret and the hashes of
  your recovery codes, and which version of these terms you accepted and when. We need them to
  give you an account, under our contract with you.
- **Where you signed in**: for every browser or device you sign in from, its internet (IP)
  address, the browser's description of itself and when it was last used, all of which the
  account page shows you, and the address each confirmation or reset link was sent to. We keep
  them to keep your account secure, which is our legitimate interest.
- **What you upload and ask**: the documents, what is read from them (their text, images of
  their pages, summaries, facts and the search index), and your chats with their questions and
  answers. We need them to provide the service.
- **Usage**: the pages, questions and model use your organisation counted, and what they cost,
  each with the member it was for, to hold the organisation to its plan and, on a paid plan,
  to bill it.
- **The audit log**: who signed in, uploaded, downloaded, deleted or shared what, and when,
  with the IP address of the request. Your organisation's owners and admins can read it, to see
  what happens to their documents, which is their and our legitimate interest. It never holds
  what a document, a question or an answer says.
- **Abuse protection**: how many requests each account, each IP address and each email address
  (as a hash only) made in a minute, to slow down password guessing and floods.
- **Server logs**: the IP address, time and path of each request, and the service's own record
  of what it did, to keep it running and to find faults. They never hold what a document, a
  question or an answer says, nor an email address.

We send you email only about your account: to confirm your address, to reset your password,
and to confirm that you asked for your account to be deleted. We send no marketing, show no
advertising, sell no personal data and build no profile of you.

## How long it is kept

- **Documents**, and everything read from them, until a member deletes them or the
  organisation is deleted.
- **Chats**, for as long as the organisation's plan keeps a chat after its last question
  ($chat_retention), unless deleted sooner.
- **Audit log entries**, for as long as the organisation's plan keeps them
  ($audit_retention).
- **Sessions and links**, until $credential_days days after they expired or were ended.
- **Abuse protection counts**, an hour.
- **Usage records**, as long as the organisation exists.
- **Your account**, until you delete it. It goes $deletion_days days after you ask, with every
  organisation of which you are the only member; in an organisation with other members, the
  usage and audit log entries about you stay with the organisation without your name and your
  IP addresses.
- **Backups**: $backups

## Cookies and storage in your browser

Emudoc sets two cookies, both needed for the service to work: `$session_cookie`, which keeps you
signed in for up to $session_days days, and `$oauth_cookie`, which lasts $oauth_minutes minutes
while you sign in with Google or Microsoft. Your browser's own storage keeps your choice of a
light or dark theme, which documents and folders you had open, how wide you made the panes, and
which notices you dismissed. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and
$third_parties so there is nothing to consent to.

## Security

- Connections to the service are encrypted, and so are its connections to anything outside its
  own network.
- Passwords are kept only as hashes, and two-factor sign-in is available.
- Every request is checked against the organisation it is for, so no organisation can reach
  another's data.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or
object to how we use it, and to have it in a machine-readable form to take elsewhere. Two of
these you can do yourself on the account page: "Download your data" gives you a copy as a file,
and "Delete account" deletes it. For anything else, write to $contact. You can also complain to
the data protection authority where you live or work, or where we are established.

Emudoc is a tool for work and is not meant for children under 16.
