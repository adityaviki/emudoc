# Getting started

Emudoc keeps your documents in projects and answers questions about them, citing the passages it
read so that you can check each answer against its source.

1. **Make a project** on the [projects page](/): one per piece of work, such as a supplier, a
   case or a deal. Everyone in your organisation sees it, unless you make it private.
2. **Upload documents** into it: drop files on the library, or use Upload. Each is read in the
   background, its status ring filling in, and is ready to search and ask about once the ring
   gives way to a green dot ([Uploading and organising documents](/help/documents)).
3. **Ask** in the chat beside the library, in your own words: "Does invoice 1043 match the
   contract's prices?" The answer cites the passages it used; click a citation to open the
   document at that passage ([Searching and asking](/help/asking)).
4. **Invite your team** from the [organisation page](/organization), on a plan with room for
   them ([Organisations, members and roles](/help/organisations)).
