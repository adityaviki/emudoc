# Your data: exports, the trash and deletion

## Exporting a project

A member exports a whole project from its card on the [projects page](/): one ZIP file with every
document as it was uploaded, in its folders, with the files it had before a new version and their
text, what was read from each (its outline, passages and facts) and the chats shared with the
project and their own. It is made in the background, the
bell says when it is ready, and it is kept $export_days days for whoever asked for it.

## A copy of your account

"Download your data" on the [account page](/account) gives you a file of what Emudoc keeps
about you: your account, the organisations you belong to, where you are signed in, your chats,
notices and usage, and the audit log entries about you.

## The trash

What a member deletes stays in the trash for $trash_days days, from which it can be restored
([Uploading and organising documents](/help/documents)).

## Deleting your account

"Delete account" on the account page deletes it $deletion_days days after you ask, with every
organisation you are the only member of; until then the app shows only the way to keep it. The
last owner of an organisation with other members hands ownership over first.

## Deleting an organisation

An owner deletes the organisation at the foot of its page, once its subscription no longer
renews. It is deleted $deletion_days days later with everything in it, and every member is told;
until then nothing can be added or changed in it, and any owner can keep it.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) says what is kept, for how long, and who receives it.
