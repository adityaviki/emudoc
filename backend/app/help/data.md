# Your data: exports, the trash and deletion

## Exporting a project

A member exports a whole project from its card on the [projects page](/): one ZIP file with every
document as it was uploaded, in its folders, with the files it had before a new version and their
text, what was read from each (its outline, passages and facts) and the chats shared with the
project and their own. It is made in the background, the bell says when it is ready, and it is
kept $export_days days for whoever asked for it.

To export some of a project's documents, choose them together in the library and choose "Export …
documents" in their menu: the ZIP file holds those documents in their folders and what was read
from them, without the chats, and the project's exports on the projects page list it.

## A copy of your account

"Download your data" on the [account page](/account) gives you a file of what Emudoc keeps about
you: your account, how you sign in, your workspace, where you signed in from, the chats you
started and the usage counted to you. The documents belong to the workspace and are not in it.

## The trash

What a member deletes stays in the trash for $trash_days days, from which it can be restored
([Uploading and organising documents](/help/documents)). A chat you delete goes into your own
trash, under "Deleted chats".

## Deleting your account

"Delete account…" on the account page deletes it $deletion_days days after you ask, and signs out
every other browser at once; until then the app shows only the way to keep it, and signing in
keeps it. Your chats go with it; the documents you uploaded belong to the team and stay. The
workspace's last owner, while it has other members, hands ownership over first ("Hand over…" in
the members list); where you are its only member, the workspace goes too.

Your team, which runs Emudoc, says what else it keeps and who receives it.
