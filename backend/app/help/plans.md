# Plans and usage

Each organisation is on a plan, which sets what it may hold and use:

$plans

Pages and questions count from the first of each month, in UTC, and start afresh on the next. A
document without pages, such as a Word file or a spreadsheet, counts a page for every
$chars_per_page characters of its text. A question counts as it is asked, whatever becomes of its
chat, and trying its answer again counts no other. The sample project counts against nothing. Figures agreed with an organisation in place of
its plan's are listed in the Plan section of its page.

## Usage

Every member reads what the organisation holds and has used of each figure, by day and by
month, on its [usage page](/organization/usage), linked from the Plan section of the
[organisation page](/organization). Owners and admins are told when the month's pages or
questions reach 80 % and all of what the plan allows.

## Reaching a limit

A request past a limit is refused with a sentence that names the figure and what lifts it:
$lifts An organisation
that holds more than its plan allows, after a move to a smaller plan or fewer seats, keeps
everything and is read-only until it is within its plan: its members read, ask, download and
delete, but add and change nothing. After a move to a plan that keeps chats or audit log
entries for less time, they are kept as before for $held_days days.
