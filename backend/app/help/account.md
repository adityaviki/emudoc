# Your account and sign-in

Your [account page](/account), "Account and security" in the menu behind your initials, changes
your name, your time zone, your email address and your password, and lists where you are signed
in, to sign any of it out. $address_change

## Signing in

A sign-in lasts up to $session_days days, or less where the workspace signs its members in again
sooner.$outside $forgotten Changing your password signs out every other browser, and a new one
is at least $password_chars characters.$breaches$pausing

## Two-factor sign-in

Turn on two-factor sign-in on the account page: scan the code with an authenticator app and enter
the code it shows, then keep the $recovery_codes recovery codes it shows somewhere safe, since
each signs you in once without the phone. The workspace's owners and admins may require it of
everyone who works in it, those who sign in through another service too.

## Notices

The bell in the header lists your notices: when a long upload is done, when a project's export
is ready, when a review is assigned to you, when you are mentioned in a comment or someone
replies in a thread you wrote in, when a question set's report is ready, when an automation's
run found something, and, once you turn it on, what arrived in your projects over the week.
Under Notices on the account page you choose, kind by kind, $notice_channels; until you choose,
the workspace's choice holds. "Also show them on this computer as they come" shows them as the
browser's own notices too. Notices are kept $notification_days days.
