# Your account and sign-in

Your [account page](/account), behind your initials in the header, changes your name, the time
zone your email is dated in, your email address (through a link sent to the new one) and your
password, and lists where you are signed in, to sign any of it out.

## Signing in

A sign-in lasts up to $session_days days, or less in an organisation that signs its members in
again sooner. A forgotten password is reset from the sign-in page, through a link sent by email,
which signs the account out everywhere.$breaches$pausing

## Two-factor sign-in

Turn on two-factor sign-in on the account page: scan the code with an authenticator app, then
keep the recovery codes it shows somewhere safe, since each signs you in once without the
phone. An organisation may require it of everyone who works in it.

## Notices

The bell in the header lists your notices from every organisation you belong to: when a long
upload is done, when a project's export is ready, and for owners and admins when the month's
pages or questions run low. Under
Notices on the account page you choose, kind by kind, whether each is told in the app, by email,
both or neither; the link at the foot of a notice's email stops that kind's email. Notices are
kept $notification_days days.

## Letting support see your account

Under Emudoc's support on the account page you can let our support see your account in the
organisation you work in, for a day or a week, and withdraw that at any time.
Support then sees what you see there, read only, $support_hours at a time; you are told by email
each time, and the organisation's audit log records it.
