# Working together

## Comments

Talk about a document where it is. **Comments**, on a document's tab, says how many threads are
open and opens them beside the text. Choose words in the text, or open a passage from a
citation, and press **New comment** to start a thread about them, or comment on the whole
document; the words chosen head the thread, and a click on them shows them in the text, after a
new reading or a new version too. Type "@" to mention a member who sees the project: they are
told who mentioned them and on which document, never your words ($told), and the members who
wrote in a thread are told of a reply. **Resolve** a thread once it is settled; the resolved
threads fold away under "Show … resolved", and "Open again" brings one back. A comment can be up
to $comment_chars characters. Everyone who reads the project comments, viewers too; you edit and
delete your own comments, and the workspace's owners and admins may delete any. The assistant does not
read comments, and a search does not find them.

## The project's activity

**Activity**, among the library's buttons, lists what was done in the project lately, newest
first: its uploads and new versions, what was deleted and restored, reviews, comments, runs of
its cross-checks and question sets, and chats shared. Each line opens what it was about
while that is still there, and one member's uploads close together are one line. Everyone who
sees the project reads it, for as long as the workspace's audit log keeps its entries.

## My files

**My files**, among the library's buttons, holds your own Word documents and Excel workbooks in
the project, which only you see: nobody else does, the workspace's owners and admins included.
"Add a file" keeps one there$my_views, up to $my_files in a project. Edit it in Word or Excel and
give it back with "Upload a new version": every version stays, to show or download. "Copy to the
library" gives the project's members a copy, as a new document or as the next version of the
document it came from, if you may change the project. My files are not searched, exported with
the project or shared by link.

The assistant writes there for you, whatever your role. Ask in a chat for a letter, a memo or a
report and it writes the Word document into your My files, its sources as numbered notes, and
the answer's step ("Wrote … into My files") opens it. Ask for a spreadsheet, a table of what the
documents say or a calculation, and it makes an Excel workbook there, with formulas where a value
comes from others, so that you can follow and change them. Ask it to change one of your files,
or a Word document or workbook of the project, which it copies into My files first, and it edits
it in place, keeping the formatting of everything it does not change. Each change is a new
version you can go back to. A workbook holding charts, pictures, pivot tables or macros it reads
but leaves to Excel to change, and it never changes the project's library.

Asked for a reply email, a dispute letter, a summary memo, a comparison table or a report, the
assistant follows the project's template of the kind. "Templates", in My files, shows each kind's
tone and parts, in order, which apply to everyone in the project; whoever may change the project
changes them, and a writing follows the template as it is at the time. A disputed document offers
"Write a dispute letter" on its tab: the assistant writes the letter into your My files from what
was read from the document and what the project's cross-checks found about it, citing where each
figure is written. It shows in My files while it is written, and with "Try again" and "Dismiss"
if it could not be.

## Share links

Someone without an account sees a document, or an answer, through a link: "Share by link" on a
document's tab, once it is indexed, or "Share the answer" under an answer. Choose how long it runs
($share_days days) and, if you like, a password of at least $password_chars characters, which you
send apart from the link, another way; then "Make a link" and copy it. A document's link shows the
document and lets its file be downloaded; an answer's shows the question, the answer and the
passages it cites, and nothing else of the project. A document or an answer has up to
$share_links links running, each listed under "Links running" with how often it was opened, and
"Revoke" stops one at once. Whoever may change the project makes links; the member who made one
revokes it, as the workspace's owners and admins may, who see every running link on the
workspace's page under Share links and can turn sharing by link off for everyone. A link stops
too when the member who made it is removed, becomes a viewer or no longer sees the document.
