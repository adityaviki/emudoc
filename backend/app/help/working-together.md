# Working together

## My files

The library has two tabs at its top. **Project files** holds the documents and folders every
member of the project sees. **My files** holds your own Word documents and Excel workbooks in
the project, which only you see: nobody else does, the workspace's owners and admins included.
"Add a file" keeps one there$my_views, up to $my_files in a project. Edit it in Word or Excel and
give it back with "Upload a new version": every version stays, to show or download. "Copy to
project files" gives the project's members a copy, as a new document or as the next version of
the document it came from, if you may change the project. My files are not searched, exported
with the project or shared by link.

The assistant writes there for you, whatever your role. Ask in a chat for a letter, a memo or a
report and it writes the Word document into your My files, its sources as numbered notes, and
the answer's step ("Wrote … into My files") opens it. Ask for a spreadsheet, a table of what the
documents say or a calculation, and it makes an Excel workbook there, with formulas where a value
comes from others, so that you can follow and change them. Ask it to change one of your files,
or a Word document or workbook of the project, which it copies into My files first, and it edits
it in place, keeping the formatting of everything it does not change. Each change is a new
version you can go back to. A workbook holding charts, pictures, pivot tables or macros it reads
but leaves to Excel to change, and it never changes the project's library.

Say how a letter or a report should read, its parts in order, its tone, what it asks for, and
the assistant writes it to that outline. To dispute an invoice, ask in a chat for a dispute letter
about it: the assistant writes the letter into your My files from what the documents say, citing
where each figure is written, and leaves what no document states, such as your name, in square
brackets for you to fill in.

## Share links

Someone without an account sees a document, or an answer, through a link: "Share by link" on a
document's tab, once it is indexed, or "Share the answer" under an answer. Choose how long it runs
($share_days days) and, if you like, a password of at least $password_chars characters, which you
send apart from the link, another way; then "Make a link" and copy it. A document's link shows the
document and lets its file be downloaded; an answer's shows the question, the answer and the
passages it cites, and nothing else of the project. A document or an answer has up to
$share_links links running, each listed under "Links running" with how often it was opened, and
"Revoke" stops one at once. Whoever may change the project makes links; the member who made one
revokes it, as the workspace's owners and admins may, who see every running link on the
workspace's page under Share links and can turn sharing by link off for everyone. A link stops
too when the member who made it is removed, becomes a viewer or no longer sees the document.
