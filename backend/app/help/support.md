# Getting help

The question mark in the header writes to our support from wherever you are: choose whether it
is a problem, a question or an idea, and say it in your own words. The message goes with your
name and address, the page you wrote from and the reference of the last request that failed
there, so that we can find what you met; the answer comes to your email address. You can also
write to $support.

A page that ran into an error shows a reference: give it when you write, and we find what
happened. When we need to see what you see, we ask you to let support see your account for a
while ([Your account and sign-in](/help/account)).
