# Freight Services Agreement

Between Acme Industrial Supplies Ltd ("the Customer") and Northwind Freight GmbH ("the Carrier"), effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Services

The Carrier carries the Customer's palletised goods by road between Felixstowe and the ports of Hamburg, Rotterdam and Antwerp, collecting within two working days of an order.

## 2. Rates

Each lane is charged at the rate of the Carrier's rate card in force on the day of collection. A fuel surcharge may be added, at most 6 % of the freight charged.

## 3. Payment

The Customer pays each invoice within thirty (30) days of its date. Late payments accrue interest at 1 % per month.

## 4. Liability

The Carrier's liability for loss of or damage to goods is limited to 8.33 SDR per kilogram of gross weight, as the CMR Convention sets.

## 5. Term

The agreement runs for twelve months and renews for twelve more unless either party gives sixty (60) days' written notice.
